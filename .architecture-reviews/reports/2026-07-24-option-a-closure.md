## Provenance review

The before-state optimized for one authority by deleting the upstream authority. Provenance showed that this would make the fork own schema, resolver, status, reply, doctor/update, validation, and SDK divergence indefinitely. Option A instead keeps upstream `modelByChannel` intact and narrows the fork profile to the three locally required non-model fields.

The measured affected-surface delta fell by 88 net lines even after adding the previously omitted pricing-cache consumer to the comparison. This is a real reduction across a more complete owner/caller surface, not a reduction obtained by excluding an indirect consumer. Current `upstream/main` still exposes and consumes `modelByChannel`, so the realigned fork follows rather than removes that contract.
