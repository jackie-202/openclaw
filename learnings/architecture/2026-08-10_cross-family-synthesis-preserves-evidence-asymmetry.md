# Cross-family synthesis must preserve evidence asymmetry

When a compatibility proposal spans many families, a detailed neighboring investigation is not evidence for an unaudited family. Build a complete family ledger first and mark missing dedicated evidence as blocked rather than inheriting a verdict from shared code, generated artifacts, or proposal prose.

Normalize heterogeneous predecessor verdicts into the final decision vocabulary only after retaining each report's original verdict, confidence, pinned revisions, and proof gaps. This prevents labels such as "obsolete" or "replace" from losing the conditions that made them safe.

For interaction analysis, model dependencies as owner-to-consumer edges and keep unknown nodes visible. An implementation-ready subset is safe only when it is dependency-closed and every excluded or operator-owned decision is explicit.
