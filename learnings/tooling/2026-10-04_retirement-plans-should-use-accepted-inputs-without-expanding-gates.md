# Keep retirement plans proportional to the remaining work

The first `calm-fork-3432` plan was rejected for adding extensive ledgers,
custody gates and external-artifact prerequisites to an already-authorized
source retirement. The supplied handoff already established external ownership,
file hashes, native-suite results and effective host isolation.

- Plan the remaining deletion, actual consumer decoupling and focused proof;
  reference the accepted manifest rather than inventing another transfer process.
- Record exact paths, scenario ownership and results in the requested checkpoint;
  avoid extra evidence documents unless execution genuinely needs them.
- Treat missing offline public-package resolution as a specific consumer-test
  dependency, not a new installed-package or deployment acceptance gate.
- Preserve the distinction between supplied native-suite evidence and worker
  execution; do not infer scenario equivalence from filenames or test counts.
- Re-read appended review feedback and remove it from the final plan artifact;
  validate the dated title and allowed section names independently.

Evidence: `plans/handoffs/deliberation-plugin-retirement/accepted-host-contract.md`
and `plans/2026-10-04_calm-fork-3432_deliberation-plugin-retire-replaced-bundled-implementation.md`.
This learning records a planning revision, not source retirement or test execution.
